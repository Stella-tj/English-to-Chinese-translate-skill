# Example: a short sentence

This is an illustrative response, not a recorded model evaluation. Equivalent wording is acceptable.

**User**

```text
翻译：We should prioritize sustainable growth.
```

**翻译**

我们应该优先考虑能长期持续的增长。

**常用词**

| 单词 / 短语 | 文中含义 | 记忆搭配（含中文） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| prioritize | 优先考虑 | prioritize tasks：按重要程度安排任务 |
| sustainable | 可持续的，能长期维持的 | sustainable growth：可持续增长 |
| growth | 增长 | steady growth：稳定增长 |
